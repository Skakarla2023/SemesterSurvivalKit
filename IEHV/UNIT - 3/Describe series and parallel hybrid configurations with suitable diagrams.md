- Series and Parallel are two different ways of connecting the IC engine and electric motor in a Hybrid Electric Vehicle.
- The main difference is:
  - Series Hybrid: The IC engine does not directly drive the wheels. The electric motor alone drives the wheels.
  - Parallel Hybrid: Both the IC engine and electric motor can directly drive the wheels.

# Series Hybrid Configuration

- In a Series Hybrid Vehicle, the IC engine is connected to a generator, and the generator produces electricity to run the electric motor or charge the battery.
- The IC engine does not directly drive the wheels. Only the electric motor drives the wheels.

### Working of Series Hybrid

1. The fuel tank supplies fuel to the IC engine.
2. The IC engine produces mechanical energy.
3. This mechanical energy drives the generator.
4. The generator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
5. The electrical energy is supplied to the electric motor through power electronics.
6. The electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
7. Finally, the motor drives the wheels through the transmission.

The battery can also supply electricity to the motor and store excess electrical energy.

**Eg:** Nissan e-power

# Parallel Hybrid Configuration

- In a Parallel Hybrid Vehicle, both the IC engine and electric motor are mechanically connected to the transmission and can drive the wheels.
- Both the IC engine and electric motor can directly provide mechanical power to the wheels.

### Working of Parallel Hybrid

1. The fuel tank supplies fuel to the IC engine.
2. The IC engine converts fuel energy into mechanical energy.
3. The battery supplies electrical energy to the electric motor.
4. The electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
5. Both the engine and electric motor are connected mechanically to the transmission.
6. Their power is combined and transferred to the wheels.

**Eg:** Honda Insight
