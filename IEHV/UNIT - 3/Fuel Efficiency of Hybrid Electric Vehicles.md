# Fuel Efficiency Analysis of Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEVs)

- A Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV) uses both an Internal Combustion (IC) engine and an electric motor to drive the vehicle.
- Fuel efficiency analysis is used to determine how effectively a hybrid vehicle uses fuel and electrical energy to travel a certain distance.
- The main aim is to:
  - Reduce fuel consumption.
  - Improve mileage.
  - Reduce exhaust emissions.
  - Use engine and motor power efficiently.
  - Recover energy during braking.

### Factors affecting fuel efficiency in HEVs

- Driving conditions: City driving with frequent stops and starts can benefit from electric assistance and regenerative braking.
- Vehicle speed: Maintaining a steady speed generally improves fuel efficiency compared with frequent acceleration and braking.
- Acceleration: Sudden acceleration requires more power and increases fuel consumption.
- Road conditions: Uphill roads require more power, while downhill roads may allow energy recovery.
- Engine efficiency: Operating the IC engine near its efficient range reduces fuel consumption.

### Influence of Power Management Strategies on Fuel Economy

- Power management means deciding how the engine, electric motor and battery should work together to meet the power demand of the vehicle with minimum fuel consumption.
- The controller continuously decides:
  - When the engine should start or stop.
  - When the electric motor should drive the vehicle.
  - When both engine and motor should provide power.
  - When the battery should be charged.
  - How much energy should be recovered during braking.
