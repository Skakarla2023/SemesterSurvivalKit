## Drivetrain configuration

- A drivetrain is the collection of components that transmits power from the engine/motor to the wheels so that the vehicle can move.
- Drivetrain configuration means how the engine, electric motor, generator, battery, and wheels are connected and arranged in a hybrid vehicle.
- Different arrangements decide how power flows from the engine and/or motor to the wheels.


### 1. Series Hybrid

- Engine → Generator → Electric motor → Wheels
- The electric motor directly drives the wheels.
- The engine mainly generates electricity.

### 2. Parallel Hybrid

- Engine → Wheels
- Electric motor → Wheels
- Both the engine and motor can directly drive the wheels.

### 3. Series-Parallel Hybrid

- Combines the series and parallel arrangements.
- Power can flow through different paths depending on the driving condition.
- It uses components such as an ICE, two electric machines and a planetary gear system.
