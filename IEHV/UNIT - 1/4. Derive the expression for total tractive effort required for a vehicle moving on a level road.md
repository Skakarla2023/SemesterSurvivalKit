## Aerodynamic Drag

<img width="600" height="385" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e96bced6-7559-4cfb-86bf-16ece6c12159" />

- Aerodynamic drag is the force that opposes a vehicle's motion as it moves through air.
- The faster the car moves, the more it has to push against the air.
- It mainly consists of 2 components:
  - Shape drag
  - Skin resistance
 
### 1. shape drag

- It is caused by the shape of the vehicle.
- High pressure forms in front of  the vehicle.
- Low pressure forms behind the vehicle.
- These pressure differences oppose the vehicle's movement.


### 2. Skin fraction

- Air close to the vehicle moves almost with the vehicle, while air farther away is relatively still.
- The difference in air speeds creates friction, producing drag.

## Grading Resistance

- Grading resistance is the resistance experienced by a vehicle when it moves uphill on an inclined road.
- It occurs because gravity pulls the vehicle downward along the slope, opposing its upward movement.
- When a car climbs a hill, gravity tries to pull it back down.
