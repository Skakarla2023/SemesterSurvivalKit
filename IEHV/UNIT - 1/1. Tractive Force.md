# Tractive force

- It is the force produced by the vehicle's driving wheels that act on the road surface to move the vehicle forward.
- When the engine produces power, it transfers torque to the driving wheels. The wheels push the road backward, and the road exerts an equal and opposite force on the wheels in the forward direction. This forward force is called tractive force.
- In simple words, tractive force is the force that helps a vehicle move forward.

### Working

1. The engine produces power.
2. Power is transmitted through the transmission system to the driving wheels.
3. The wheels rotate and push the road surface backward.
4. The road provides a forward reaction force on the wheels.
5. This forward force moves the vehicle.

#### Understanding the motion

- If tractive force is greater than resistive forces → the vehicle accelerates.
- If tractive force equals resistive forces → the vehicle moves at constant speed.
- If tractive force is less than resistive forces → the vehicle slows down.


### Significance of Tractive force

**1. Starting the vehicle** : Tractive force helps the vehicle overcome its initial resistance and start moving from rest.

**2. Acceleration** : It helps increase the speed of the vehicle. Greater tractive force generally produces greater acceleration when the opposing forces remain the same.

**3. Climbing hills** : When a vehicle travels uphill, tractive force helps overcome the component of vehicle weight acting down the slope.

**4. Overcoming resistance** : 

Tractive force is required to overcome various forces opposing vehicle motion, such as:
- Rolling resistance – resistance due to tyres rolling on the road.
- Air resistance – resistance caused by air acting against the moving vehicle.
- Gradient resistance – resistance due to the slope of the road.
- Mechanical resistance – friction in vehicle components.
