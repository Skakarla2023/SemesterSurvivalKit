# Mathematical Model of Vehicle Performance

- A mathematical model of vehicle performance is a set of mathematical equations used to predict how a vehicle will behave under different driving conditions.
- We use formulas to calculate how fast a vehicle can go, how well it can climb a hill, and how quickly it can accelerate.
- The performance of a vehicle depends mainly on:
  - Maximum cruising speed
  - Gradeability
  - Acceleration performance
- Imagine a vehicle moving forward, several forces try to stop the car. They are:
  - rolling resistance
  - aerodynamic drag
  - Grading resistance
  - Acceleration resistance
