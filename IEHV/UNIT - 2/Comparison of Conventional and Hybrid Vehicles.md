| Feature                  | Conventional Vehicle                                     | Hybrid Vehicle                                                 |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Power source**         | Petrol or diesel engine                                  | Engine + electric motor                                        |
| **Fuel consumption**     | Generally higher                                         | Generally lower                                                |
| **Fuel efficiency**      | Lower                                                    | Higher                                                         |
| **CO₂ emissions**        | Higher                                                   | Generally lower                                                |
| **Air pollution**        | Higher                                                   | Generally lower                                                |
| **Energy recovery**      | Usually no regenerative braking                          | Uses regenerative braking                                      |
| **Battery**              | Small battery mainly for starting and electrical systems | Larger battery used for electric propulsion                    |
| **Engine operation**     | Engine runs for most driving                             | Engine can switch off or operate less in suitable conditions   |
| **Noise**                | Higher, especially at low speeds                         | Lower when operating on electric power                         |
| **Initial cost**         | Generally lower                                          | Generally higher                                               |
| **Maintenance**          | Conventional engine maintenance                          | More complex because it has both engine and electrical systems |
| **Driving range**        | Usually good because of fuel tank                        | Usually good because it can use both fuel and electric power   |
| **Environmental impact** | Generally higher                                         | Generally lower during operation                               |
| **Technology**           | Simpler and well established                             | More advanced and complex                                      |
