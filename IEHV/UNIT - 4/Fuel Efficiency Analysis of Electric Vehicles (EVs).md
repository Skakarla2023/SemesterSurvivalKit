# Fuel Efficiency Analysis of Electric Vehicles (EVs)

- Fuel efficiency in an Electric Vehicle (EV) refers to how efficiently the electrical energy stored in the battery is converted into motion to drive the vehicle.
- Unlike conventional vehicles that use petrol or diesel, EVs use electrical energy stored in a battery. This energy is supplied to the electric motor, which drives the wheels.
- Higher energy efficiency means the vehicle travels more distance using less electrical energy.

> The energy flow in an EV is:

```txt
Electricity from charging
          │
          ▼
     Battery Pack
          │
          ▼
  Power Electronics
   (Inverter)
          │
          ▼
   Electric Motor
          │
          ▼
   Transmission
          │
          ▼
       Wheels
          │
          ▼
    Vehicle Motion
```

2. Vehicle weight
3. Driving speed
