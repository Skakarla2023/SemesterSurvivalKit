# DriveTrain Topology

- A drive-train is the system of components that transfers power from the motor to the wheels to move the vehicle.
- Drive-train topology means the different ways these components are arranged and connected.
- Drive-train configuration defines how power sources(like motor, Internal combustion engine) and mechanical components(gearbox, clutch, axles) are connected to drive the torque to the wheels.
- There are six types of drive train topologies based on drive train configurations. They are:

### (a) Conventional drive with multi-gear transmission

- The electric motor produces mechanical power.
- The clutch connects or disconnects the motor from the transmission.
- The gearbox contains multiple gears to change speed and torque.
- The differential distributes power to the driving wheels.

### (b) Single-gear drive without clutch

- The motor is connected to the transmission through a single gear ratio.
- A separate clutch is not required.
- The single gear reduces motor speed and increases torque.
- The differential transfers power to both driving wheels.

### (c) Integrated motor, fixed gear and differential

- The motor, fixed reduction gear and differential are combined into a compact drive arrangement.
- The fixed gear reduces motor speed and increases torque.
- The differential allows the two wheels to rotate at different speeds while turning.

### (d) Two-motor drive with fixed gears

- Two separate electric motors are used.
- Each motor drives one wheel through a fixed reduction gear.
- The speed and torque of each motor can be controlled independently.

### (e) Two-motor direct wheel drive with fixed gears

- Two separate motors are used, each connected to a driving wheel.
- Each motor uses a fixed gear for speed reduction.
- The motors are placed close to the respective wheels, reducing the need for a long mechanical transmission.

### (f) Gearless in-wheel motor drive

- Two separate electric motors are placed directly inside the wheels.
- Each motor drives its wheel directly.
- No gearbox or fixed reduction gear is used.
- Each wheel can be controlled independently.
