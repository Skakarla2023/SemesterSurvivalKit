# Electric Traction

- Electric traction is the system of using electrical energy to produce mechanical power for driving a vehicle.
- In simple words, electric traction means using an electric motor to move a vehicle.

**Examples:**
```
Electric cars
Electric buses
Electric trains
Electric scooters
Electric bikes
```
- In an Electric Vehicle (EV), electrical energy is stored in a battery. The battery supplies power to an electric motor, which rotates the wheels and moves the vehicle.

### Major Componenets

**1. Battery Pack**

- It is the main energy storage system of an EV.
- Stores electrical energy in chemical form.
- Supplies DC electrical power to the motor through the power electronics.

**2. Power Converter (Power Electronics)**

- It controls and converts electrical power supplied by the battery.
- It mainly consists of:
  - Inverter: Converts DC power from the battery into AC power for an AC motor.
  - DC-DC Converter: Converts one DC voltage level into another.
  - Motor Controller: Controls the motor's speed and torque by regulating electrical power.

**3. Electric Motor**

- Converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
- Produces rotational motion.
- Drives the vehicle wheels.
- Can also act as a generator during regenerative braking.

**4. Transmission System**

- Transfers mechanical power from the motor to the wheels.
- Adjusts speed and torque according to driving requirements.
- EVs commonly use a single-speed reduction gear.

**5. Differential**

- Transfers power to the driving wheels.
- Allows the left and right wheels to rotate at different speeds while turning.

**6. Vehicle Controller**

- Acts as the brain of the EV.
- Receives input from the accelerator and brake pedals.
- Controls motor speed and torque.
- Monitors vehicle operation and coordinates power flow.

**7. Charging System**

- Supplies electrical energy to recharge the battery.
- Converts power from the external charging source into the required battery charging form.

**8. Regenerative Braking System**

- Recovers kinetic energy during braking.
- The electric motor acts as a generator.
- Converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
- Sends the recovered energy back to the battery.


### Working

1. The battery stores electrical energy.
2. When the driver presses the accelerator, the controller receives the signal.
3. The controller sends signals to the power converter.
4. The power converter supplies controlled electrical power to the motor.
5. The motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
6. The transmission system transfers the power to the wheels.
7. The wheels rotate and move the vehicle.
8. During braking, regenerative braking recovers some energy and stores it in the battery.
