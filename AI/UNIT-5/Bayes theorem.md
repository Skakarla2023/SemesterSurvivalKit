# Bayes theorem

- Bayes theorem is a method used to update the probability of something being true, when new information is given.
- Bayes theorem is a fundamental principle in AI that calculates the probability of an event based on prior knowledge and new evidence.
- It is used to update beleifs and make predictions using new evidence.

  - P(B|A): Probability of occurence of event B given that event A has already occured.
