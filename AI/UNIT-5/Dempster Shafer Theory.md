# Dempster Shafer Theory

- Uncertainity arises when there is lack of knowledge(knowledge is unavailable).
- DST is a method used in AI to handle uncertainity.
- DST extends probability theory by seperating beleif and probability.
- There are three main terms in Dempster Shafer Theory.
  1. Belief
  2. Plausibility
  3. Evidence

### Belief:

- It is something that you think is true or real.

### Plausibility:

- This is about how likely something is to be true.

### Evidence:

- The facts or evidence that supports a belief.

### Frame of Discernment:

- It is the complete set of all possible outcomes in DST.
