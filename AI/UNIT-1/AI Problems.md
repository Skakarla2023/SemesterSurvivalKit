# Problems in AI

- An AI Problem is a task that requires intelligent behavior to solve-that cannot be solved using simple rules.
- AI problems usually involve:
    - Huge Data
    - Uncertainity
    - Making Decisions
    - Learning from experience
    - Reasoning


 # History of AI
