# Agile Model

- It is a software development approach where the project is built in small cycles(sprints or iterations) instead of delivering the whole project at once.
- The main  focus is on flexibility, customer feedback and rapid delivery of the software.
- Agile model is developed as a response to the disadvantages of traditional models like the waterfall model, where changes are difficult once the development begins.

## Core Idea

- Develop the software incrementally in small cycles.
- Get Cntinuous feedback from the user.
- Be flexible to make changes even in coming stages of the development.


## Agile Principles

1. High customer satisfaction
2. Deliver working software frequently.
3. Face-to-face communication
4. Encourage self-organising teams
5. Welcome changing requirements
6. Collaboration between developers, testers and customers
7. Maintain a sustainable pace of development.


## Agile Frameworks

- Scrum
- Kanban
- Lean Development
- Extreme development


## Differences between Waterfall Model and Agile Model


| Flexibility                          |  WaterFall            | Agile                           |
|--------------------------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------------|
| Approach                             | sequential            | Iterative and incremental       |
| Customer involvement                 | Less                  | high                            |
| Testing                              | at the end            | continuous(every sprint)        |
| Delivery                             | at the end of project | continuous(after every sprint)  |


## Advantages

- High quality sofware.
- Continuous feedback- high customer satisfaction.
- Teams become more motivated and collaborative.
- Flexible to changing requirements.
