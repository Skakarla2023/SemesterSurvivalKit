# ☂️ Umbrella Activities

- Unlike the core activities of Software development which are directly involved in the development of a software i.e., designing, implementing, deployment, umbrella activites are the support activities that happen throughout the design process from the first idea until the software is delivered and maintained.
- They don't build the project, they are supporting activities that help keep the project on track, organised and on full-quality.
- They are called umbrella activities because just as the umbrella protects you from the rain, these activities cover every phase of the development.
- They make sure project runs smoothly and we deliver the best quality software.
- Following are th umbrella activities:
  - Software project tracking and control
  - Formal technical reviews
  - Software quality assurance
  - SCM or software configuration management
  - Document preparation and production
  - Reusability management
  - Measurement and metrics
  - Risk Management
