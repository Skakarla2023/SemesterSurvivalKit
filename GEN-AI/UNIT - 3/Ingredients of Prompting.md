# Prompt

- A prompt is a specific instruction or question or text input given to Artificial Intelligence, to direct its output.
- It is like a message or instruction that tells the AI what to create, how to behave and which context to consider.
- A prompt needs to be more clear and specific so that one gets a response closer to their expected response.


## Ingridients of prompting

### 1. Context

- Context gives the AI the background information needed to understand the task properly.
- It helps the AI give a more relevant and accurate response.

**Eg:**

> "I am a beginner learning Java. Explain inheritance in simple words."

- Here, "I am a beginner learning Java" is the context.

### 2. Task Objective

- The task objective tells the AI exactly what it needs to do.
- It should be clear and specific.

**Eg:**

> "Summarize this article in 5 bullet points."

### 3. Target Audience

- Target audience tells the AI who the response is intended for.
- It helps the AI adjust the complexity and language of the response.

**Eg:**

> "Explain Artificial Intelligence to a group of 10-year-old students."

### 4. Tone or style

- Tone or style tells the AI how the response should sound or be written.
- Common styles include formal, friendly, professional, simple, or humorous.

**Eg:**

> "Explain this topic in a friendly and simple tone."

### 5. Examples

- Examples show the AI what kind of response or pattern is expected.
- They help the AI understand the desired output more clearly.

**Eg:**

```
"Happy → Positive
Angry → Negative
Excited → ?"
```

### 6. Role

- Role tells the AI to act as a particular person, expert, or professional.
- It helps the AI provide a response from a specific perspective or expertise.

**Eg:**

> "Act as a Java teacher and explain inheritance to a beginner."

### 7. Output Format

- Output format specifies how the response should be presented.
- It can specify formats such as bullet points, tables, paragraphs, JSON, or numbered lists.

**Eg:**

> "Explain the advantages of AI using 5 bullet points."
