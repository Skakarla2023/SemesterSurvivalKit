# Zero Shot Classification

- Zero-Shot Classification is a technique in which a model classifies text into categories without being specifically trained on labelled examples for that classification task.
- The model uses the knowledge and language patterns it learned during its pretraining to understand the input and determine which category best matches it.
- Instead of training the model with thousands of positive and negative movie reviews, we provide the text and the possible categories.
