# RAG

- RAG stands for retrieval augmented generation.
- It is a technique that combines information retrieval with LLM(large language models) for getting more accurate answers and context-based answers.
- Instead of answering only from the knowledge using pretraining, the LLM first retrieves information from a relevant external source and then uses that info to generate its answer.
- RAG = Retrieve information + Give it to the LLM + Generate an answer.


### Why is RAG needed?

LLMs have some limitations:
- They may provide incorrect information or hallucinate.
- Their training data may be outdated.
- They may not know private or company-specific information.
- They cannot automatically know the latest information in a document or database they have not been given.

RAG helps solve these problems by providing relevant information before the LLM generates its answer.
