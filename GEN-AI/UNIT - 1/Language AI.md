## Language AI

- Language AI is the part of Artificial Intelligence that helps computers understand and work with human language.
- Normally, humans can easily read a sentence, understand its meaning, translate it, or reply to it. Computers cannot naturally do this. Language AI gives computers the ability to process human language.
- For example:
  - When ChatGPT answers a question, it is using Language AI.
  - When Google Translate translates English to Telugu, it is using Language AI.
  - When a voice assistant understands what you say, it is using Language AI.
 
### History of Language AI

- Language AI has developed step by step. In the beginning, computers could not learn language by themselves. Humans had to give them fixed rules.
- Later, computers started using statistics and probability to find patterns in language. Then Machine Learning and Deep Learning allowed computers to learn more complex patterns from large amounts of data.
- A very important development happened in 2017, when the Transformer architecture was introduced. Transformers became the foundation for modern language models such as BERT and GPT.
- Later, Large Language Models (LLMs) became more powerful, and the release of ChatGPT in 2022 made Generative AI widely popular.

**Language AI over the years**

- **1950** : Early ideas of AI and turing test were introduced.
- **1960-70** : Rule based systems were developed, such as **ELIZA**.
- **1980-90** : Probability and statistics were used for understanding complex patterns.
- **2000** : Machine Learning improved language-related tasks.
- **2010** : Deep learning and Neural networks improved language understanding.
- **2017** : The development of transformer architecture became the foundation for LLMs.
- **2018** : Models such as **BERT** and **GPT** became more important in Language AI.
- **2022** : chatgpt and generative AI made language AI popular among general users.
