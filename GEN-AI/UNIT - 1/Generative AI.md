## Generative AI

- Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can create brand-new content, such as text, images, music, or videos.
- Unlike traditional AI, which is built to analyze existing data or make predictions (like filtering spam emails or recommending a movie), generative AI learns patterns from massive amounts of data and then uses those patterns to make something entirely new.

### Types of Content Generated

Generative AI can create different types of content:
- **Text**: Stories, articles, emails, summaries, answers
- **Images**: Drawings, photographs, designs
- **Audio**: Music, speech, sound
- **Video**: Short videos and animations
- **Code**: Programs and software code

### Examples

- **ChatGPT** – Generates and understands text.
- **Image generation models** – Create images from text prompts.
- **AI coding tools** – Generate and explain programming code.
- **AI music tools** – Generate music and audio.


### Applications of Generative AI

Generative AI is used in many fields:
1. **Education** – Creates notes, explanations, quizzes, and study material.
2. **Software Development** – Generates code, documentation, and test cases.
3. **Business** – Creates emails, reports, advertisements, and other content.
4. **Healthcare** – Helps summarize information and assist in research.
5. **Entertainment** – Generates stories, music, images, and videos.
