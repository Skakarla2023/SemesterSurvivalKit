## Structure of BNN

- A Biological Neural Network (BNN) is a network of interconnected biological neurons in the brain and nervous system.
- A neuron receives information from other neurons, processes it, and passes the information to other neurons.
- The basic flow is:

```
Dendrites → Cell Body → Axon → Synapse → Dendrites of Next Neuron
```

### Dendrites – Receiving information

- Dendrites are the input part of a neuron.
- They receive signals from nearby neurons or from sensory receptors.
- For example, when you see something, sensory information eventually reaches neurons, and neurons receive these signals through their dendrites.

```
Dendrites = Receive information
```

### Cell Body (Soma) – Processing information

- The cell body, also called the **soma**, contains the nucleus and performs important functions of the neuron.
- It receives and combines the signals coming from the dendrites.
- The neuron essentially "decides" whether the incoming signals are strong enough to produce an electrical pulse.

```
Cell body = Processes and combines incoming information
```


### Axon – Carrying information

- The axon is a long, thin structure extending from the cell body.
- When the neuron reaches the required threshold, it produces an electrical impulse.
- This impulse travels along the axon away from the cell body.

```
Axon = Carries the signal away from the neuron
```

### Axon Terminals – Passing the information

- At the end of the axon, there are small branches called axon terminals.
- These terminals communicate with other neurons through structures called synapses.

```
Axon terminals = Pass the signal to the next neuron
```

### Synapse – Communication point

- A synapse is the junction or communication point between two neurons.
- There is a very small gap between the sending neuron and receiving neuron called the synaptic cleft.
- The electrical signal reaching the axon terminal causes chemical neurotransmitters to be released.
- These neurotransmitters cross the synaptic cleft and reach the next neuron.

```
Synapse = Communication point between neurons
```
