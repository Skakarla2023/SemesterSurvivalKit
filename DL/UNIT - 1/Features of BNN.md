## Biological Neural Network

- A BNN is a network of neurons(nerve cells) present in the human brain or nervous system.
- All the neurons are interconnected and they communicate with each other through electric and chemical signals.
- Becuase of this connection, our brain can recognise patterns, make decisions and respond to different situations.
- The following are the characteristics of BNN:
  - Robustness and fault tolerance
  - Flexibility
  - Ability to deal with variety of data sources
  - Collective competence

### 1. Robustness and fault tolerance

- Robustness means that the neural network can continue working even when some parts of it are damaged or not working properly.
- In the human brain, there are a very large number of neurons connected with each other. If some neurons die or stop functioning, the overall performance of the brain usually does not decrease significantly.
- This property is called fault tolerance.


### 2. Flexibility

- A biological neural network is flexible, meaning it can adapt itself to new situations and environments.
- It does not need a programmer to give it instructions for every new situation.
- The brain can learn from experience and surroundings and change its response accordingly.
- brain learns automatically from experience.


### 3. Ability to Deal with a Variety of Data Situations

- Biological neural networks can handle different types of information, even when the information is not perfect.
- According to the notes, a neural network can deal with information that is:
  - **Fuzzy** – not completely clear or precise
  - **Probabilistic** – based on possibilities
  - **Noisy** – contains unwanted or incorrect information
  - **Inconsistent** – information may not always agree with each other
 

### 4. Collective Computation

- This is one of the important characteristics of biological neural networks.
- In a BNN, many neurons work together at the same time to perform a task.
- Instead of depending on one neuron to perform the complete calculation, thousands or millions of neurons participate in processing information.
- This is called collective computation.
