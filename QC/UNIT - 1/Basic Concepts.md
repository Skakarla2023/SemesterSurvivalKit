## Quantum Computing

- It is a type of computing based on the principles of quantum mechanics.
- Unlike classical computers which uses bits(0 or 1), quantum computers use quantum bits(qubits) to store and process information.
- A classical bit can have only one value at a time 0 or 1.
- A qubit can be in 0,1 or a linear combination of both, this ability is called **superposition**.


### Qubits

- A qubit (quantum bit) is the basic unit of information in quantum computing.
- Unlike a classical bit, which can only be 0 or 1, a qubit can have : 0,1 or linear combination of both(the ability to exist in linear combination of 0 and 1) is called **superposition**.
